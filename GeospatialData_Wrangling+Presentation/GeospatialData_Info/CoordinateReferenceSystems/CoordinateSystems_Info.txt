###################################
Coordinate Reference Systems (CRS)
    Geographic CRS
    Projected CRS (PCS)
##################################

Think of CRS as the broad umbrella term for any system that defines how coordinates relate to real-world locations on Earth. Under that umbrella, there are two main categories:
    • Geographic CRS (GCS): Uses a 3D curved surface and angular units (latitude and longitude in degrees). Example: EPSG:4326.
    • Projected CRS (PCS): Takes a GCS and flattens it onto a 2D plane using linear units (meters or feet). Example: EPSG:3857 (Web Mercator).

Because every Projected CRS relies on a Geographic CRS underneath it to know where it sits on the 3D Earth, EPSG:4326 is often the starting foundation for many other coordinate systems.



EPSG (European Petroleum Survey Group)
-----------------------------------------
a registry of Coordinate Reference Systems



EPSG:4326
----------
* the code for **WGS 84**, the standard geographic CRS based on latitude and longitude in degrees. It's what GPS uses and what most web maps use for raw coordinates.

* Coordinates are in **decimal degrees**, with longitude (x) first in Earth Engine, e.g. `[-122.082, 37.42]`

* It is *not* projected, so a "pixel" is a slice of degrees, not a fixed distance on the ground.


**What this means in your export using ee CLI:**

<<< python
ee.batch.Export.image.toDrive(
    image=image,
    crs="EPSG:4326",
    scale=30,          # meters, but see below
    ...
)
>>>

When you use `crs="EPSG:4326"`, the `scale` is converted to degrees internally (about 30 m ≈ 0.00027°). Pixels are not square on the ground: they get narrower east-west as you move away from the equator. If you need equal-area or equal-distance measurements, use a projected CRS instead.



**Alternatives for Mexico**
  
EPSG:326xx` / `EPSG:327xx`: 
    UTM zones (north/south), in meters. Good for local analysis.

    EPSG:32614` (UTM zone 14N): 
        Between 102°W and 96°W, northern hemisphere between equator and 84°N, onshore and offshore. Canada - Manitoba; Nunavut; Saskatchewan. Mexico. United States (USA). Projected


`EPSG:6372`: 
    Mexico - onshore and offshore. Projected
    Mexico's national CRS (ITRF2008 / LCC)

**Tip:** if you leave `crs` out, Earth Engine uses the image's native projection, which is often the best choice unless you need a specific one. You can check it with `image.projection().getInfo()`.
