The Sentinel-2 constellation of satellites was designed to provide data for precision agriculture. Peer-reviewed research has indicated \
that such remote sensing data can help produce more sustainable management of farming practices and improvements in yield for farmers. \
And yet the detailed algorithms for taking Sentinel-2 data for a specific location and processing the GeoTIFF files to obtain useful \
agricultural information is lacking. Please do the following:\
1. Review open access peer-reviewed journals, relevant textbooks, and other legitimate sources (eg, the UN's FAO) for detailed \
procedures and metrics for using remote sensing data from satellites or drones. Clearly, the mean NDVI and the CV of local Moran's I \
will be important metrics. The output from this item should be a .pdf file summarizing the findings and listing only the most notable \
or important references.\
\
2. Provide detailed step-by-step explanations and procedures for the processing of Sentinels-2 data through Google Earth Engine and \
Python into practical information for the management of small-scale crop operations. You do not need to write the execution code, just \
indicate what metrics are used; the type of data extraction, processing, and analyses that needs to be performed at each step; and an \
explanation of how the final results are to be interpreted. More specifically, the step-by-step procedures should address the following \
questions, \
     * How are abiotic and biotic stress detection measured and evaluated in crops using satellite and/or drone data? What are the metrics and how are they interpreted? \
     * How are abiotic and biotic stress detection measured and evaluated at the agroecosystem level with satellite and/or drone data? What are the metrics and how are they interpreted? \
     * How is agroecosystem resilience measured and evaluated with satellite and/or drone data? What are the metrics and how are they interpreted? \
     * How is crop productivity measured and evaluated with satellite and/or drone data? What are the metrics and how are they interpreted? \
     * How can satellite and/or drone data be used for the management of crop variability? What are the metrics and how are they interpreted? \
     * How can satellite and/or drone data be used for maintaining, improving, and forecasting crop yields? What are the metrics and how are they interpreted? \
     * How can satellite and/or drone data be used for measuring and evaluating ecosystems services in agroecosystems? What are the metrics and how are they interpreted? \
     * How can satellite and/or drone data be used for measuring and evaluating non-crop plant diversity in agroecosystems? What are the metrics and how are they interpreted? \
The outputs from this item should be in .txt file format and organized per question. That is, there should eight (8) .txt files that \
contain detailed step-by-step explanations and procedures (one .txt file per question) for each of the questions posed in Item 2 above.
