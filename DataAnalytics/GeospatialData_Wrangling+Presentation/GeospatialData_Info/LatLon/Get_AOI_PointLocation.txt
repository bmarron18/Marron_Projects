############################
Find Area of Interest (AOI)
###############################
1. Go to geojson.io (https://geojson.io/)
  2. Zoom to your farm using satellite imagery, select the Polygon tool, and draw each field boundary.
  3. Click the polygon or use the table tab on the right to add properties (field_id, crop, sowing_date, harvest_date, treatment).
  4. Click Save → GeoJSON (NOT Shapefile).

# Convert local GeoJSON directly into an Earth Engine FeatureCollection with geemap
fields_fc = geemap.geojson_to_ee("path/to/your_fields.geojson")


############################
Pinpoint Lat/Lng of any place!!
############################

1. Go to SpatialReference.org
https://spatialreference.org/


2. Use the intractive map to pinpoint the location (map uses Geographic CRS (GCS) ==> EPSG:4326)
	# UMA per EPSG:4326
	# Center grassy area in front of bldgs
LatLng(19.190319, -100.088689)


3. Select a Projected CRS (PCS) and pinpoint the location
	# UMA per EPSG:32614 (UTM zone 14N)
	# Center grassy area in front of bldgs
	# UTM position as easting and northing w/in UTM zone 14N
Easting: 	385526.4 	<== (m)
Northing: 	2122245.4 	<== (m)

	# Converted to decimal degrees (DD)
LatLng(19.190335, -100.088829)
